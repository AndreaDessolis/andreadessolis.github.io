What's new in HDX Video Edit

Versions from the most recent. The same notes are in the program, in Help.

Back to HDX Video Edit

Version 1.0.2, November 18, 2026

What's new

  1. Search sounds, music and videos (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+O, Timeline menu): sound effects and music with a free licence from Openverse (the effects from the large Freesound collection) and old videos from Internet Archive, free of charge and with no account. Type in your own language: the program translates the words into English by itself and tells you what it searched for. Space plays a preview without downloading; Enter downloads to the project's Downloads folder, or adds it straight to the timeline (sounds as a background, videos as a clip). Only files free of everything appear or, if you choose, also those that ask you to credit the author.
  2. Credits: next to each downloaded file the program writes where it comes from, who made it and under which licence. "Copy the credits for YouTube" (File menu) copies the lines of the timeline's files that ask you to credit the author, ready to paste into the video description.

Version 1.0.1, November 17, 2026

What's new

  1. Fit to screen (Ctrl+Alt+R), for YouTube Shorts and phone videos: a clip with a different shape from the timeline is fixed in words, with no calculations. Fill the screen (in the centre, on the left or on the right), Whole with a blurred background, Slowly pan from one side to the other, or black bars. With Follow the subject, artificial intelligence finds who is singing, speaking or playing, and the framing follows them as a camera operator would. In the Settings you can also choose what to do with added clips of a different shape.
  2. Record start and stop, in the Speed window: at the beginning the clip starts from still, at the end it slows down to a stop, video and sound together, with the pitch rising and falling like a turntable (up to 10 seconds).
  3. Measure the clip (Ctrl+Alt+M) and Measure the timeline (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+M): the sound (loudness in LUFS, peak, range, tone) and the picture (light, crushed shadows, blown highlights, colours, sharpness), in a window to read calmly, with the advice calculated by the program when needed.
  4. Match the sound of the clips (Ctrl+Alt+U): clips recorded in different places reach the same loudness and a similar tone (the new Matched tone effect, with 8 bands), measured as they will sound in the video, also through the effects of the whole timeline. It can be done on all the clips or only on the selected one, leaving the rest as it is.
  5. Project advice (Ctrl+Alt+F1): artificial intelligence looks at the clips, knows the measurements of sound and picture and the Guide, and tells you what to improve and how, with the right commands and keys; or it answers your question about the project, such as where to put the logo or how to add a title. It never changes anything by itself.
  6. Clip audio: now you can also raise the volume (up to +12 dB), and the exact value is always shown.
  7. In the Settings, "Audio prepared in advance for listening": no more clicks at the changes between one clip and the next, even with videos on a slow disk or on pCloud.

Fixes

  1. Keep the pitch: on a sped-up or slowed-down clip that was not the first in the timeline, the sound was taken from the wrong point of the file.

Version 1.0.0, November 15, 2026

What's new

  1. The first version of HDX Video Edit, the video editor designed to be used entirely with a screen reader (NVDA and JAWS): everything is done with the keyboard, every action is announced, and the video is shown large for whoever assists you.
  2. Editing: videos and photos in a list, to cut at the point you are listening to, split, join, move, rename and replace, with Undo and Redo always available.
  3. Clip speed, from 0.25 to 2 times, keeping the pitch of the voice or changing it like a tape.
  4. The section: mark the start and end with F9 and F10, listen to it in a loop with F11, and export it, cut it out or keep only it.
  5. 69 transitions between clips, plus openings and closings from black, white or a colour.
  6. Audio effects (equalizers, compressor, noise reduction, reverb and many more) and video effects (light and colour, zoom and framing, text and titles, logo, blurring of faces and number plates), on one clip or on the whole timeline, with ready-made chains for the most common cases.
  7. Background music under the clips you choose, with the volume of each clip under control.
  8. Export to MP4 (H.264, H.265, AV1) or audio only (MP3, WAV, FLAC), also of a single clip or of the section, and YouTube chapters ready to paste.
  9. Artificial intelligence (Gemini or OpenAI): it describes the image, finds faces and number plates to hide, and with Ctrl+Shift+F1 answers your questions about how to use the program.
  10. Complete Settings: start with Windows, minimize to the notification area, projects and exports folders, automatic saving, changeable shortcuts, announcements by voice or with a sound; appearance with the colours of the logo; Italian, English and Spanish.
  11. Automatic updates, with signature checking and a return to the previous version if something goes wrong.
