What's new in HDX VM

Versions from the most recent. The same notes are in the program, in Help.

Back to HDX VM

Version 1.5.0, October 17, 2026

New

  1. The Voicemeeter recorder (Banana and Potato), in the new Recorder menu: start or stop recording (Ctrl+R), pause (Ctrl+Shift+R), length (Ctrl+D), open the recordings folder, choose what to record and in which format, and play a file on the outputs you want (for example to let people in a call hear it).
  2. Network audio (VBAN), in the new Network menu: turn VBAN on and manage the 8 incoming and outgoing streams to send and receive audio between computers.
  3. MIDI controllers: the buttons of a USB controller can run your switches, faders and knobs can set the volume of channels, and the button lights show the state (for example mute). Choose it in Settings, in the new MIDI controller category.
  4. The level inside the mixer: Ctrl+L tells how much comes out of the channel and Ctrl+Shift+L how much goes into the source, without leaving the mixer.
  5. ASIO audio interfaces: with an ASIO interface on A1, in Properties you choose its channels for the physical sources and for the outputs A2 to A5.
  6. New switches: start or stop recording, pause it, turn VBAN on or off, volume with a controller fader.

Improvements

  1. The main window is simpler: only the Voicemeeter status and the two lists. Send to, Properties and the Voicemeeter commands are in the menus and in the row's context menu.
  2. The mixer opens straight away even on microphones.

Good to know

  1. All the new features are explained in the Guide (F1): the recorder in chapter 8, network audio in 9, MIDI controllers in 10.

Version 1.0.0, October 16, 2026

New

  1. First official version of HDX VM, the program to control Voicemeeter in a fully accessible way with your screen reader. All features are explained in the Guide (Help, Program guide, or F1).

Good to know

  1. To use HDX VM you need Voicemeeter (Standard, Banana or Potato), to be installed separately from the VB-Audio website.
