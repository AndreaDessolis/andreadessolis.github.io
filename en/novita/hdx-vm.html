Version 1.0.0, October 16, 2026

New

  1. First official version of HDX VM, the program to control Voicemeeter in a fully accessible way with your screen reader. All features are explained in the Guide (Help, Program guide, or F1).

Good to know

  1. To use HDX VM you need Voicemeeter (Standard, Banana or Potato), to be installed separately from the VB-Audio website.
