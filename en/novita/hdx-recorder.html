What's new in HDX Recorder

Versions from the most recent. The same notes are in the program, in Help.

Back to HDX Recorder

Version 1.0.1, October 29, 2026

New

  1. The new Favorites bank, besides Effects, Jingles and Backgrounds: ten keys with the sounds you always want at hand, taken from the other banks (for example four effects, three jingles and three background tracks). Each key plays as in its own bank: the jingle goes into the live log, the background track crossfades in. In the control room, on the bank list, the Applications key opens the menu of the row with "Add to Favorites"; in Favorites, Delete empties the key. Each configuration has its own Favorites.
  2. Shift+F12 tells you which bank is selected right now, without changing it (also in the Sources menu, Sound pads).

Improvements

  1. The program remembers the selected bank: when you open it again you start from that one.

Version 1.0.0, October 28, 2026

New

  1. First official version of HDX Recorder, the program to record audio and video and go live on a web radio, fully accessible with your screen reader. All the features are explained in the Guide (Help, Program guide, or Ctrl+F1).

In short

  1. Many sources together, each at its own volume: microphones, computer audio, a single program, background files, Internet radio and sound pads.
  2. Many formats, with the mix and, if you want, a track for each source; the pre-start check, the level test and the clipping warning.
  3. Mixer, effects for each source, final mix and scenes to recall with a key.
  4. Video of the screen, of a window or of the webcam, with composition and helpers for tutorials.
  5. Live broadcasts on web radio with the control room: track list, jingles, pads, on-air break, preview, headphone monitor and returns for connected guests.
  6. Scheduled recordings, artificial intelligence with your own Gemini or OpenAI key, announcements by voice or with sounds, automatic updates.
