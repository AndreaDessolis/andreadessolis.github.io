What's new in HDX Recorder

Versions from the most recent. The same notes are in the program, in Help.

Back to HDX Recorder

Version 1.1.4, November 10, 2026

New

  1. You can now record from audio interfaces with ASIO® drivers: the Add source menu has an ASIO audio interface item. It works with professional audio interfaces and with programs that use ASIO, such as REAPER with ReaRoute and Voicemeeter, in mono or stereo, with several interfaces at once too.
  2. ASIO is a registered trademark of Steinberg Media Technologies GmbH.

Version 1.1.3, November 8, 2026

New

  1. The Window source can now also carry the audio of its program: just tick the Also include this program's audio box. Video and audio stay on a single line, with volume, mute, Mixer, headphones and effects, with no need to add a second source.

Version 1.1.2, November 6, 2026

New

  1. You can now translate HDX Recorder into your language: the Help menu has a Translate the program item. You translate one phrase at a time, your work saves itself and you can pick it up whenever you like.
  2. With the Try it in the program button you hear your phrases in their place right away, without changing anything. At the end the program prepares the file to send to the author.

Version 1.1.1, November 4, 2026

Fixes

  1. When the program opened, the Pause button appeared even with no recording in progress: now it is there only while you record, as intended.

Version 1.1.0, November 4, 2026

New

  1. Live streams on YouTube with your account (YouTube menu, YouTube account): before going live you choose title, description, visibility, made for kids, thumbnail, latency, rewind for viewers and embedding on other sites. The program creates the live stream on the channel, gives you the link and closes it by itself at the end; if it never went on air, it removes it. It also works with the stream key alone, without an account.
  2. The live chat, read aloud as soon as messages arrive (Ctrl+Shift+F9 turns it on or off; Ctrl+F9 repeats the last message); Ctrl+F10 opens the window to read it and reply, with deleting, blocking and moderators. From the YouTube menu: polls in the chat, the list of moderators and the Super Chats received.
  3. Scheduled live streams on YouTube: Schedule a live (day, time and all the options), Scheduled lives to edit them, copy their link or delete them; before going live you can start on one of them.
  4. My videos: edit title, description, visibility, thumbnail and other settings (tags, category, languages, translations of title and description, license, altered content, paid promotion), publish now or at a set time, delete, add to a playlist.
  5. Upload a video to the channel, in the background, with the upload status and the option to cancel it.
  6. My playlists (create, edit, reorder, remove videos) and My channel (channel numbers, description, keywords, country, language, trailer, banner image and watermark).
  7. A new look with the HDX Recorder colours: midnight blue, purple and red, rounded panels and buttons, turquoise level bars. You choose it in Settings, General, Appearance (Modern or Classic); with Windows high contrast the system colours stay.
  8. A window with what's new at the first start after the update.

Improvements

  1. Normalization and compressed copy: a tick every 2 seconds while they work, and every minute the step and the percentage aloud, also with Ctrl+T. A second recording waits in the queue instead of blocking the program. Exiting while they work, the program asks for confirmation (the preselected answer is No), and updates wait until they have finished. Tick and voice are set in Settings, Announcements, "Waiting for normalizations and copies".
  2. The Pause button appears only while recording, like the Pause item in the menus.
  3. In the Control room the Bank and Scenes lists start from the first row.

Fixes

  1. Exiting while a recording was stopping by itself, the recording could be stopped twice.
  2. If at startup another program (for example the antivirus) was locking the settings file, the settings could be lost: now the program retries and, if it can't, it writes nothing over it.
  3. Recording names forbidden by Windows (CON, NUL, COM1...) and part files left over from old recordings no longer stop the recording.
  4. In the scheduled recording window the Name field was taller than it should be and ended up behind the other controls.

Version 1.0.3, November 1, 2026

New

  1. The on-air log (the .csv file with the time of songs, themes and jingles) is now optional and off by default: no more extra files in the recordings folder. If you use it, for example for music rights reports, turn it on in Tools, Settings, Recordings category, with the "Save the on-air log" check box.

Version 1.0.2, October 31, 2026

New

  1. Radio live broadcasts with a secure connection (SSL): some services require it, and without it you could not go on air. In the radio settings, next to the port, there is the "Secure connection (SSL)" check box; if the address starts with "https://" the program uses it even without the check box. The on-air title also goes through the secure connection.
  2. In the Help menu, Join L'angolo di HDX on Telegram: the group of all my programs, where you can ask for help, suggest ideas and stay up to date. It opens the invite link, in Telegram or in the browser.

Fixes

  1. With a server that requires a secure connection, the connection test said everything was fine and then the live broadcast kept retrying forever ("the station closed the connection"). Now the program notices it and says so clearly: "The server uses a secure connection (SSL): in the radio settings, check Secure connection (SSL)".

Version 1.0.1, October 29, 2026

New

  1. The new Favorites bank, besides Effects, Jingles and Backgrounds: ten keys with the sounds you always want at hand, taken from the other banks (for example four effects, three jingles and three background tracks). Each key plays as in its own bank: the jingle goes into the live log, the background track crossfades in. In the control room, on the bank list, the Applications key opens the menu of the row with "Add to Favorites"; in Favorites, Delete empties the key. Each configuration has its own Favorites.
  2. Shift+F12 tells you which bank is selected right now, without changing it (also in the Sources menu, Sound pads).

Improvements

  1. The program remembers the selected bank: when you open it again you start from that one.

Version 1.0.0, October 28, 2026

New

  1. First official version of HDX Recorder, the program to record audio and video and go live on a web radio, fully accessible with your screen reader. All the features are explained in the Guide (Help, Program guide, or Ctrl+F1).

In short

  1. Many sources together, each at its own volume: microphones, computer audio, a single program, background files, Internet radio and sound pads.
  2. Many formats, with the mix and, if you want, a track for each source; the pre-start check, the level test and the clipping warning.
  3. Mixer, effects for each source, final mix and scenes to recall with a key.
  4. Video of the screen, of a window or of the webcam, with composition and helpers for tutorials.
  5. Live broadcasts on web radio with the control room: track list, jingles, pads, on-air break, preview, headphone monitor and returns for connected guests.
  6. Scheduled recordings, artificial intelligence with your own Gemini or OpenAI key, announcements by voice or with sounds, automatic updates.
