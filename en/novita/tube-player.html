What's new in Tube Player

Versions from the most recent. The same notes are in the program, in Help.

Back to Tube Player

Version 1.1.0, October 25, 2026

New

  1. In the results, for every video, when it was published ("3 days ago"); once the video has been prepared, the exact date ("published October 23, 2026").
  2. Next to "Search in" two new boxes: "Sort by" (relevance or view count) and "Uploaded" (last hour, today, this week, this month, this year), to find the latest videos on a topic.
  3. Settings, General: "YouTube content language", to choose the language of titles, descriptions and results (by default the program's language).
  4. Esc inside a channel, a playlist or a collection goes back to the previous list, like Backspace (if a video is playing, the first Esc stops it).

Fixes

  1. The videos of many Italian channels showed titles translated into English: now they come in the chosen language, also in the names of downloaded files.
  2. Esc pressed while a video's details were still loading said "No video playing": now it cancels opening the details.

Version 1.0.0, October 25, 2026

New

  1. First version of Tube Player: search YouTube for videos, channels and playlists, with "Search in" to choose what to search for; the list of results grows by itself when you reach the end.
  2. Channels and playlists like File Explorer folders: Enter to go inside, Backspace or the "Back to" button to go back; inside a channel Ctrl+1, Ctrl+2 and Ctrl+3 for videos, playlists and live streams.
  3. The player with all its commands reachable with Tab: position, pause, forward and back, volume, speed, picture on or off, video details. The main keys work everywhere.
  4. Continuous playback with Ctrl+Enter, with next and previous video.
  5. The video details with Alt+Enter: date, description and chapters to start the video from.
  6. Video and audio downloads in many formats, also in the original format, with the downloads window (Ctrl+J).
  7. Collections: lists you make yourself with videos, channels and playlists (Ctrl+S to save, Ctrl+K to open them).
  8. Spoken announcements and sounds for everything, fully customizable shortcuts, three languages (Italian, English, Spanish).

Good to know

  1. The first time it starts, Tube Player downloads by itself from their official websites the engine it needs to read YouTube (yt-dlp, Deno and mpv) and keeps it up to date once a day. FFmpeg, needed for some downloads, is downloaded at the first download that needs it.
  2. This is the first version with automatic updates: from now on Tube Player tells you by itself when a new version is available.
