What's new in Studio Player

Versions from the most recent. The same notes are in the program, in Help.

Back to Studio Player

Version 1.2.1, November 7, 2026

New

  1. Karaoke now plays your audio tracks too (MP3, FLAC and the others). While you sing the other songs, the program prepares the backing track without vocals in the background; if a song's turn comes before it is ready, it starts with the vocals and the program tells you, and the vocals disappear by themselves as soon as the backing track is ready.
  2. The lyrics of audio tracks arrive by themselves: from an LRC file next to the track, from the timed lyrics written inside the file (as karaoke backing track programs add them) or from the internet, from the free LRCLIB archive. Only title, artist and length are sent to LRCLIB; if you prefer not, turn off Search for lyrics on the internet in Settings, Karaoke category.
  3. The lyrics lines are timed to the singer's real voice, so they arrive exactly when needed. If the lyrics belong to another recording of the song, the program tells you.
  4. Ctrl+Shift+F searches for lyrics by hand, with title and artist already filled in: the lyrics you choose take priority over all the others and are remembered.
  5. In the Karaoke menu, Save the lyrics as LRC saves them next to the track with the same name: other players can read them too, for example the one on your phone.
  6. Ready-made karaoke backing tracks are recognised by themselves and sung as they are, with their backing vocals, without separating them. For the others there is This track is already a backing track, yes or no, in the Karaoke menu.
  7. F2 says the line to sing again, Ctrl+F2 starts again from the line being sung.
  8. A short sound marks the exact moment each line starts, and during long breaks you hear how long until you sing.
  9. Ctrl+N gives the song the name of who sings it: you read it in the queue, and when the song starts you hear Marco's turn. If you like, the queue goes in turns, one song each per round.
  10. Music between songs: when nothing is playing in karaoke, a folder of tracks of your choice plays softly, and fades away when the next song begins.
  11. Big lyrics for the audience: Ctrl+Alt+K shows them full screen on a connected TV or projector, or in a window if there is only one screen. It never takes the focus, so with your screen reader you will not notice it.

Fixes

  1. Chords written in the middle of the syllables of some KAR files are no longer read: for example you no longer hear "che sogno sudaticA cio" but "che sogno sudaticcio".
  2. In karaoke, Play from the menu and double-clicking the queue follow the queue rules, like Enter.
  3. When you remove the song being sung from the queue, its lyrics leave the list.
  4. The Playback menu in karaoke no longer has the items that were of no use there: Previous track, Repeat and Shuffle.
  5. In the combo boxes of the karaoke panel F4 opens the list, as everywhere in Windows.
  6. Leaving karaoke you hear a single announcement.

Good to know

  1. Preparing the backing tracks of audio tracks needs the stem engine, the same one used for stems. Without it, audio tracks are sung with the vocals.

Version 1.2.0, November 7, 2026

New

  1. Karaoke: Ctrl+K turns the window into a complete karaoke for KAR and MIDI files. Type the title, press Enter twice and sing: Search, Songs found, Queue and Lyrics are the only four Tab stops.
  2. The lyrics arrive ahead of time, by as many seconds as you choose: the cursor follows the lines and the screen reader reads them before your turn. F4 turns the reading on and off.
  3. Guide vocal: the program finds the vocal channel by comparing the lyrics with the notes, and Shift+F4 turns it on and off. When a song starts it is off, so you get the backing track.
  4. Options panel with Alt+Shift+K: guide vocal, pitch, speed, lead time, reading, automatic start, reverb and chorus, which apply at once even while you sing.
  5. A song queue, with automatic start of the next song if you want it. Your track list is set aside and comes back when you leave karaoke.
  6. Karaoke lyrics clean themselves up: double copies, symbols, hyphens, overlong lines, credits and chords. Many files that showed no lyrics before now show them. Your files are never changed.
  7. New Karaoke category in Settings: the song folders and the lyrics clean-up choices.

Fixes

  1. If you installed the stem engine with an older NVIDIA card and get separation errors, you are now offered a reinstall with the right version before separating.
  2. Accents and apostrophes in the lyrics of MIDI files now read correctly.

Good to know

  1. This is the first version of karaoke: for now it works with KAR and MIDI files. More features will come with the next updates, and suggestions are welcome.
  2. Ctrl+K used to turn on the reading of the lyrics: now it opens karaoke.

Version 1.1.1, November 6, 2026

Fixes

  1. When installing the stem engine, Studio Player first checks the NVIDIA card and driver and downloads the right version. With older cards, such as the GTX 900 and GTX 1000, the previous version gave an error during separation. If you already have that error, reinstall the engine.
  2. If the card or the driver is too old, the program says so before installing and uses the processor.

Version 1.1.0, November 5, 2026

What's new

  1. Guitar chord fingerings: Ctrl+Shift+D tells you how to play the chord you hear, string by string; press it again for the next position. In the Chords window just press D.
  2. The chord and scale dictionary (Alt+Shift+D): choose a chord or a scale, read its positions on the neck string by string and listen to them.
  3. The suggested capo for playing the song's chords with the easiest shapes, in the Chords window.
  4. The next chord announced in advance, one to four beats early, by voice or with a sound.
  5. The new Chords and scales category in the Settings: chord and note names (previously in General), fingers by name or by number, the chord in advance.

Fixes

  1. With the window opened maximized, some lists stayed white instead of taking the colors of the chosen appearance.

Version 1.0.0, November 4, 2026

What's new

  1. The first version of Studio Player, the player for listening to and studying music, fully accessible with the screen reader.
  2. Independent speed and pitch, the A-B loop with saved loops, the speeding-up loop and the loop sequence, markers.
  3. The metronome, on its own or on the song's beats, with a count-in before starting.
  4. The chords and key of the song, scales for improvising and the notes of the stems.
  5. Stem separation (vocals, guitar, bass, drums and the rest) with the mixer.
  6. MIDI files with sound banks, the channel mixer, instruments to change and karaoke.
  7. The same volume for all songs, parametric equalizer, stereo and channels, night listening.
  8. Videos in the player window, with the audio track, subtitles read aloud and chapters.
  9. The format converter and the export of the song as you hear it, also each stem in its own file.
  10. Automatic updates, the backup of the settings and the complete guide in the Help menu.
