What's new in Studio Player

Versions from the most recent. The same notes are in the program, in Help.

Back to Studio Player

Version 1.8.0, November 13, 2026

New

  1. The lyrics editor, with Alt+Shift+F (also in the Studio menu, the Karaoke menu and the track's context menu): write, fix and time the lyrics of an audio track by hand. A single list of lines, each with its time: N writes a new line right in the list (Enter, and the next line opens already, so you write the whole song in one go), E edits the line you are on, Ctrl+V pastes a whole text copied from a website (an LRC with times too), Delete deletes, Ctrl+Up and Down Arrow move the words, Ctrl+Z undoes. Enter listens from that line, Space pauses, F2 says the line again. It also opens from karaoke, on the queue's song, and when you exit you are back where you were. The Save button is reached with Tab; Alt+Shift+F closes it, as it opened it.
  2. The lyrics saved in the editor come before all others, in karaoke and outside, and no automatic work changes them any more; they are read when every line has its time. If you later look for other lyrics with Ctrl+Shift+F, the program asks before replacing them. In Settings, Song lyrics category, you can also save the finished lyrics as an LRC file next to the track, for other players.
  3. Timing by ear in the lyrics editor: while the track plays, press B when the line begins. The screen reader stays quiet (you only hear a small tap) and the list stays still; when you pause, the cursor goes to the next line to tap. Right and Left Arrow move the time by a tenth of a second, with Shift by half a second. V plays only the singer's voice, if the track has stems. The hand's reaction time is subtracted by itself (200 milliseconds to start with, set in Settings, Song lyrics) and follows the speed if you slow the track down.
  4. The aligner in the lyrics editor: the lines you time yourself become fixed (F fixes or unfixes them), and A has the precise aligner time all the others, without ever moving the fixed ones. So you only need to tap a few lines by ear in the hard spots and the rest falls into place. Uncertain lines say "to check", lines with a time before a line above "out of order": C and Shift+C take you to them. P tries it: the track starts and the screen reader reads the lines as you wrote them, even before saving.
  5. The MIDI editor, with Alt+Shift+M: to play live, every track can send another program or instrument, for example Reaper, Program Changes, Control Changes and notes at precise moments of the track. So Reaper changes sounds and parameters by itself in the different parts of the song. The port is chosen in Settings, MIDI category, separate from the one that plays MIDI files; for a program on the same computer you need a virtual MIDI port, for example with the free program loopMIDI. It works for audio tracks and MIDI files, and every track remembers its moments.
  6. In the MIDI editor each line is a moment, on a bar and beat (with the beats of the metronome's "Follow the track") or at a precise time, with all the messages it sends together: for example "Bar 17, beat 1, 0:42.3, Chorus: PC 5 channel 1, CC 7 100 channel 1". N creates a moment, E edits it in a small window with boxes set with the arrows (type, channel, program and bank, controller with the names of the most used ones, value, note, velocity, duration), M sends the messages at once so you hear whether they are right, Shift+M turns all notes off, T says where you are and how long to the next moment. B creates a moment by ear while the track plays, with the messages copied with Ctrl+C; the arrows move the moment by a beat (with Shift by a bar), G snaps it to the beat or leaves it at its time, Ctrl+V pastes the copied messages into a new moment.
  7. The MIDI events start by themselves every time the track plays: in the playlist, in karaoke and in the editor (there even before saving). Every moment is prepared a moment earlier and arrives on time, even if you slow the track down. When the track starts, when you move or when the loop starts again, Reaper at once receives the last Program Change and the last value of each Control Change of that point, so it always has the right sounds; notes turned on by the events turn off by themselves if you pause or move. If you like, a small warning one beat before every moment (Settings, Announcements, Study group; off to start with).
  8. Karaoke: in the options panel (Alt+Shift+K), in "Screen for the lyrics", the new choice "This screen, full screen". With a single screen, Ctrl+Alt+K puts the big lyrics full screen on the computer, so whoever sings reads them well even from far away. The keyboard always stays in Studio Player.

Version 1.7.0, November 12, 2026

New

  1. The precise lyrics aligner. Lyrics found on the internet are often timed on another recording of the song, with a longer or shorter intro, and can arrive seconds early or late. The aligner listens to the vocal stem, recognizes the sounds of the words the singer sings and puts every line back in its place, even when the lyrics were off by many seconds. The words always stay those of the lyrics: the aligner only finds where they fall. Turn it on in Settings, Karaoke category, with With the precise aligner. It works on your computer, with the stem engine, and the first time it meets lyrics in a language (Italian, English or Spanish, recognized from the words) it downloads its model, about 1.2 GB.
  2. The aligner only uses the timing it is sure of: a line takes the new time when the aligner recognizes it well, or when the place it finds agrees with the internet lyrics. The other lines are placed between the sure ones nearby and on the voice attacks. If there are too few sure lines, for example in rap, the lyrics stay as they were: never worse than before.
  3. In karaoke the aligner works in the background on the queue, one song at a time, when the song has its lyrics and backing track ready and no stems are being separated. If it finishes while the song plays, the lyrics are fixed right away. The result is remembered: next time it is ready.
  4. Lyrics timed by the aligner are read outside karaoke too, while you listen, with the same rules as karaoke. Outside karaoke the aligner never works by itself: only what it has already done, or what you ask it, is used.
  5. Find the lyrics and time them: a new item in the list's context menu. For the selected tracks (all of them with Ctrl+A) that already have separated vocals, the program finds the lyrics, first on the computer and then on the internet if you allow it, and times them with the aligner. On the internet, lyrics of a recording a bit longer or shorter than yours are fine too, up to 30 seconds, because they are then timed on the voice. At the end you hear a summary: how many timed, already ready, without lyrics found, without separated vocals. Ctrl+Shift+E also says how many lyrics are left.
  6. The Separate the stems window has a new checkbox, Also find the lyrics and time them, when the choice separates the vocals. When the separations are done, the program prepares the lyrics too. So you can prepare a whole folder for studying, with stems and timed lyrics, without going through karaoke. It works with Ctrl+E, with Separate the stems in the context menu and with Separate the stems of all the tracks; the choice is remembered.
  7. Separation for karaoke: in Settings, Karaoke category, choose whether karaoke separates only vocals and backing track, as before, or all the parts. With all the parts karaoke removes only the vocals and every song you sing also has all the instruments ready for studying; it takes about three times the space and more time.
  8. In the track's row, if you choose it in Settings, Playlist category, with Show in the row whether the track has stems and lyrics: "stems" when the track has at least one separation ready and "lyrics" when it has lyrics that are read in time. So you see right away which tracks you can already study with. The program remembers it for each file and updates it by itself when you separate a track or time its lyrics.
  9. When you exit with separations or lyrics still to do, Studio Player asks whether to exit anyway, because exiting cancels them.

Version 1.6.0, November 11, 2026

New

  1. Playlists: in the track list, the Applications key or Shift+F10 opens the context menu. With Add to playlist you pick one of your saved playlists and the track, or all those selected with Shift and the arrows or with Ctrl+A, goes to the end of that playlist without opening it; tracks already there are not added twice. New playlist creates a playlist with only the selected tracks. The same menu also has Play, Play after, Open the track's folder, Separate the stems, Remove from the list and Properties.
  2. Properties: the last item of the context menu opens a window with all the track's data, one line per item to read with the arrows. File (name, folder, format, size, date), Track (title, artist, album, year, genre, composer, track number, comment, and whether there are lyrics, timed or not, and where they come from), Audio (duration, encoding, sample rate, bits, channels, bit rate), MIDI (tempo, time signature, channels and instruments, karaoke lyrics) and Study (remembered speed and pitch, saved loops, markers, stems, recordings). The buttons Copy all and Open the track's folder. With several tracks selected, a summary: how many, total duration, space and formats.
  3. Playlists: they open and save in the Scalette folder, inside the program folder; in Settings, Playlist category, you can choose another one. The open playlist's name goes in the window title, before or after Studio Player, or not at all, as you choose. In the Playlist menu, with an empty list only Open playlist and Recent playlists remain.
  4. Playlists: if you change the open playlist and then open another one, clear the list, open files in place of the list or exit, the program asks whether to save the changes.
  5. Playlists: Export as text creates a text file with the numbered tracks, the duration of each and the total, to print or send to the other musicians. Copy the tracks to a folder copies them, for example to a USB stick, in the playlist's order with the number before the name, and puts the playlist with the new names next to them.
  6. In the track's row, if you choose it in Settings, Playlist category, also the format: MP3, WAV, FLAC, MIDI... The duration now appears also for tracks without a title.
  7. Stems of many tracks at once: from the Study menu, Stems, Separate the stems of all the tracks, or from the context menu on the selected tracks (all of them with Ctrl+A). You choose only once, with the time expected for all, and the tracks are separated one after another in the list's order. The program skips MIDI files, tracks already separated that way, those already queued and files it can't find, and tells you a summary. Exiting with separations still to do, it asks whether to exit anyway.
  8. Lyrics of audio tracks are read also outside karaoke, while you listen, when their timing is already right for that file: timed lyrics written inside the file, as in MP3 karaoke backing tracks, or the LRC file saved by Studio Player. Outside karaoke nothing is searched on the internet.

Version 1.5.1, November 11, 2026

New

  1. Tablature: MusicXML scores (.musicxml and .mxl) now open too, the format saved by almost every notation program, such as MuseScore, Finale and Sibelius. Open them with Ctrl+O in the Tablature window, like Guitar Pro files: every staff becomes a track, with voices, lyrics, chords, sections, repeats and tempo changes. If the score is for guitar with the tuning written in it, you can also read it as tablature, with string and fret.
  2. Tablature: Shift+N chooses how the track is read, as notes (the name and octave, for example D 4) or as tablature (string and fret). By default guitar and bass are read as tablature and every other instrument as notes; your choice is remembered for each file and each track. Tracks without strings, such as piano and voice, are always read as notes, and the drums always say the name of the hits. N gives you the other reading: with notes, string and fret; with tablature, the note names.
  3. Tablature: Period and Comma let you read the row one piece at a time instead of hearing it all at once: period goes to the next piece, comma to the previous one. The pieces are the bar, the chord, every note (string and fret, or the name), the duration, every technique on its own, the pick stroke, the lyrics and the section. At the end you hear "End of the row", at the start "Start of the row"; on another row you start again from the first piece. Both commands are also in the Tablature menu.

Fixes

  1. Tablature: Space sometimes did not pause the song: it happened if, while it was playing, you had moved with the arrows onto a spot without notes. Now Space always pauses the song that is playing, and when the arrows bring you onto a rest the song stops, just as when you reach a note.

Version 1.5.0, November 11, 2026

New

  1. Studio Player now works with ASIO drivers, the ones of professional audio interfaces: Steinberg has approved and signed the agreement that allows me to use ASIO technology in my programs. In Settings, Audio devices, first choose the driver (WASAPI shared, WASAPI exclusive or ASIO); with ASIO then the interface, the music outputs and the instrument input. The ASIO card control panel button opens the driver settings. With ASIO the latency is only the buffer of your interface, and the music, the metronome and everything you hear go through the same interface, always in time.
  2. The instrument input can be mono (one input) or stereo (a pair, as on the interface: 1 and 2, 3 and 4...), both with ASIO and with WASAPI.
  3. Instrument monitoring (with ASIO): Ctrl+Shift+U turns on and off hearing your instrument or voice in the headphones, together with the song, directly inside the interface driver. Alt+Shift+Up and Down arrow adjust its volume.
  4. Recordings (with ASIO): Alt+Shift+R, or Study menu, Recordings, opens the list of the song's recordings, the newest at the top. Inside, single keys are enough: R records and stops, Space plays the song with the recording, the arrows move from one recording to another and you hear it right away at the same point of the song, Enter plays it back from the beginning, B lets you hear the song only. Every recording stays tied to the point of the song where you played it, and the program removes the interface latency by itself.
  5. In recordings: Ctrl+Up and Down arrow adjust the recording volume, Alt+Up and Down arrow the volume of the song below it; M marks it as best, F2 gives it a name, Delete sends it to the Recycle Bin, E exports it, alone or already mixed with the song as you hear it. With the loop on, L chooses between one pass only (it records one pass and stops) and all passes (each pass becomes a recording); C turns on the count-in before recording. Esc does not leave, so it cannot happen by mistake: you leave with Alt+Shift+R.
  6. New Recordings category in Settings: the loop, what happens when the recording ends (it stops ready to be heard, or you hear it right away), whether R with the song stopped starts the song, the count-in, the starting volumes and the latency correction, in case your interface driver declares it not quite right. The start and end sound is chosen in Announcements.
  7. Separate outputs (with ASIO): the metronome with the count-in, and instrument monitoring, can go to other outputs of the interface, for example only to the headphones and not to the PA. In Settings, Audio devices, first choose the type (same as the music, a stereo pair or a mono output) and then the output.
  8. Each stem and each channel of a MIDI file can also go to its own output: in the mixer, on the stem row, the Applications key (or Shift+F10) opens the new stem menu, with mute, solo, instrument and Output: main, a pair or a mono output. The choice applies to that stem in all songs, or only to the open song. For example, live: the backing tracks to the PA, the drums to the sound engineer, the metronome in the headphones.
  9. MIDI on an external module: in Settings, MIDI, Play MIDI with chooses the SoundFont bank, as before, or a Windows MIDI port, that is an external sound module or the driver of a program. With the port Studio Player sends the notes to the module at the right time: pause, seeking, loops, speed and pitch work, and the mixer controls the module (volume, mute, solo, position). A channel's instrument is chosen from the module's General MIDI list, and the Module latency keeps it in time with the metronome.
  10. These are big new features: if you notice anything wrong, feel free to write to me from Help, Contact me, or in the L'angolo di HDX group. Every report helps me.

Version 1.4.0, November 10, 2026

New

  1. Tuner: Ctrl+U enters and leaves, also from the Study menu. Play a string and you hear a tick on the left if it is flat and on the right if it is sharp, further to the side the further you are, in the centre when you are close. When the string is in tune you hear the confirmation sound and the tick stops.
  2. The tuner recognizes the string by itself and tells you; with the arrows you choose a specific string, Home goes back to automatic. There are 13 tunings for guitar, bass and ukulele, chromatic mode for any note (C) and the tuning of the open tablature. A changes tuning, Space plays the right note, F2 says note and deviation in cents, L the input level.
  3. New Tuner category in Settings: precision for the confirmation (1, 2 or 3 cents), reference A from 430 to 450 Hz, sound of the reference note and volume. What it says and the confirmation sound are chosen in Announcements.
  4. This is the first version with the tuner: if you notice anything wrong, or a tuning you are missing, feel free to write to me from Help, Contact me, or in the L'angolo di HDX group. Every report helps me make it more precise.
  5. New Audio devices category in Settings, with all the cards in one place: music output and driver (WASAPI shared or exclusive), program sounds output, and the new input, that is the microphone or audio interface with the channel the instrument is plugged into. The Test the input level button listens for 3 seconds and tells you whether the sound arrives well.

Fixes

  1. Study menu: Tablature now has the letter B, because T was also used by Stems.

Version 1.3.1, November 9, 2026

New

  1. Tablature, new keys on the grid, without opening the mixer: M mutes the chosen track, S solos it, R clears all mutes and solos.
  2. T and Shift+T go to the next and previous track, staying in the same bar; if the song is playing, the cursor keeps following it.
  3. N says the note names of the event, taking tuning and capo into account. E turns on or off the techniques said with the note, A the sound while moving. The same items are in the Tablature menu, under Tracks and options.

Version 1.3.0, November 9, 2026

New

  1. Guitar Pro tablature, from version 3 to 8, with your screen reader: Ctrl+G enters tablature, Ctrl+O opens one, and they can also be added to the track list.
  2. Move as on the fretboard: Right and Left Arrow go from one event to the next (a note, a chord or a rest), Up and Down Arrow change string. Page Up and Down move by bar, Ctrl+B goes to a given bar, Ctrl+Right and Left Arrow move by section.
  3. Hear what you land on: moving plays the note or chord of the chosen track only. Space replays the event, Shift+Space the bar, Enter starts all tracks from where you are and the cursor follows the music.
  4. The song plays in its real order, with repeats, alternate endings, Da Capo, Segno and Coda, and with techniques: hammer-on and pull-off, bends, slides, vibrato, palm mute, harmonics, grace notes, whammy bar and more.
  5. Practice on tablature: a mixer with track names, to mute your part and play along; loops on an event or bar with F9, F10 and Shift+F11; speed and metronome as usual.
  6. Alt+Shift+B says tuning, capo, time signature and BPM, D all event details, L the song lyrics, F4 chooses what to say while playing. The program resumes the last tablature where you left off; Ctrl+W closes it.
  7. New Tablature category in Settings: eighths and sixteenths or quavers and semiquavers, reversing the string order and other choices.

Fixes

  1. MIDI: starting from a precise point right after opening the file now works, instead of starting over from the beginning.

Version 1.2.3, November 8, 2026

New

  1. New Song lyrics category in Settings: choose how early the lyrics line arrives, in karaoke and, separately, outside karaoke. The values can follow the tempo of the track (a quarter of a beat, half a beat, one, two or four beats) or be fixed, in milliseconds or seconds. There are also values to make the line arrive a little later.
  2. In Settings, Playlist category, the new When a track starts, stay on the list checkbox: the cursor does not move to the Pause button, handy when you have many tracks. It is off by default.

Fixes

  1. Tell me the chords while playing now controls everything: when it is off, the program says no chords at all, even with The next chord in advance set. If you only used the advance setting, turn this checkbox on to keep hearing the chords.

Version 1.2.2, November 7, 2026

Fixes

  1. MIDI files no longer skip with large sound banks, for example Musyng Kite, and the highest sound quality: after a few seconds the audio kept breaking up. The program now uses more processor cores to create the sound and has plenty of headroom.

New

  1. If a computer still cannot keep up, the program lowers the MIDI sound quality by itself, only for that track, so the sound does not skip. The next track starts again from the quality you chose. If you want to know when it happens, turn on Announce when the quality lowers by itself in Settings, MIDI category.

Version 1.2.1, November 7, 2026

New

  1. Karaoke now plays your audio tracks too (MP3, FLAC and the others). While you sing the other songs, the program prepares the backing track without vocals in the background; if a song's turn comes before it is ready, it starts with the vocals and the program tells you, and the vocals disappear by themselves as soon as the backing track is ready.
  2. The lyrics of audio tracks arrive by themselves: from an LRC file next to the track, from the timed lyrics written inside the file (as karaoke backing track programs add them) or from the internet, from the free LRCLIB archive. Only title, artist and length are sent to LRCLIB; if you prefer not, turn off Search for lyrics on the internet in Settings, Karaoke category.
  3. The lyrics lines are timed to the singer's real voice, so they arrive exactly when needed. If the lyrics belong to another recording of the song, the program tells you.
  4. Ctrl+Shift+F searches for lyrics by hand, with title and artist already filled in: the lyrics you choose take priority over all the others and are remembered.
  5. In the Karaoke menu, Save the lyrics as LRC saves them next to the track with the same name: other players can read them too, for example the one on your phone.
  6. Ready-made karaoke backing tracks are recognised by themselves and sung as they are, with their backing vocals, without separating them. For the others there is This track is already a backing track, yes or no, in the Karaoke menu.
  7. F2 says the line to sing again, Ctrl+F2 starts again from the line being sung.
  8. A short sound marks the exact moment each line starts, and during long breaks you hear how long until you sing.
  9. Ctrl+N gives the song the name of who sings it: you read it in the queue, and when the song starts you hear Marco's turn. If you like, the queue goes in turns, one song each per round.
  10. Music between songs: when nothing is playing in karaoke, a folder of tracks of your choice plays softly, and fades away when the next song begins.
  11. Big lyrics for the audience: Ctrl+Alt+K shows them full screen on a connected TV or projector, or in a window if there is only one screen. It never takes the focus, so with your screen reader you will not notice it.

Fixes

  1. Chords written in the middle of the syllables of some KAR files are no longer read: for example you no longer hear "che sogno sudaticA cio" but "che sogno sudaticcio".
  2. In karaoke, Play from the menu and double-clicking the queue follow the queue rules, like Enter.
  3. When you remove the song being sung from the queue, its lyrics leave the list.
  4. The Playback menu in karaoke no longer has the items that were of no use there: Previous track, Repeat and Shuffle.
  5. In the combo boxes of the karaoke panel F4 opens the list, as everywhere in Windows.
  6. Leaving karaoke you hear a single announcement.

Good to know

  1. Preparing the backing tracks of audio tracks needs the stem engine, the same one used for stems. Without it, audio tracks are sung with the vocals.

Version 1.2.0, November 7, 2026

New

  1. Karaoke: Ctrl+K turns the window into a complete karaoke for KAR and MIDI files. Type the title, press Enter twice and sing: Search, Songs found, Queue and Lyrics are the only four Tab stops.
  2. The lyrics arrive ahead of time, by as many seconds as you choose: the cursor follows the lines and the screen reader reads them before your turn. F4 turns the reading on and off.
  3. Guide vocal: the program finds the vocal channel by comparing the lyrics with the notes, and Shift+F4 turns it on and off. When a song starts it is off, so you get the backing track.
  4. Options panel with Alt+Shift+K: guide vocal, pitch, speed, lead time, reading, automatic start, reverb and chorus, which apply at once even while you sing.
  5. A song queue, with automatic start of the next song if you want it. Your track list is set aside and comes back when you leave karaoke.
  6. Karaoke lyrics clean themselves up: double copies, symbols, hyphens, overlong lines, credits and chords. Many files that showed no lyrics before now show them. Your files are never changed.
  7. New Karaoke category in Settings: the song folders and the lyrics clean-up choices.

Fixes

  1. If you installed the stem engine with an older NVIDIA card and get separation errors, you are now offered a reinstall with the right version before separating.
  2. Accents and apostrophes in the lyrics of MIDI files now read correctly.

Good to know

  1. This is the first version of karaoke: for now it works with KAR and MIDI files. More features will come with the next updates, and suggestions are welcome.
  2. Ctrl+K used to turn on the reading of the lyrics: now it opens karaoke.

Version 1.1.1, November 6, 2026

Fixes

  1. When installing the stem engine, Studio Player first checks the NVIDIA card and driver and downloads the right version. With older cards, such as the GTX 900 and GTX 1000, the previous version gave an error during separation. If you already have that error, reinstall the engine.
  2. If the card or the driver is too old, the program says so before installing and uses the processor.

Version 1.1.0, November 5, 2026

What's new

  1. Guitar chord fingerings: Ctrl+Shift+D tells you how to play the chord you hear, string by string; press it again for the next position. In the Chords window just press D.
  2. The chord and scale dictionary (Alt+Shift+D): choose a chord or a scale, read its positions on the neck string by string and listen to them.
  3. The suggested capo for playing the song's chords with the easiest shapes, in the Chords window.
  4. The next chord announced in advance, one to four beats early, by voice or with a sound.
  5. The new Chords and scales category in the Settings: chord and note names (previously in General), fingers by name or by number, the chord in advance.

Fixes

  1. With the window opened maximized, some lists stayed white instead of taking the colors of the chosen appearance.

Version 1.0.0, November 4, 2026

What's new

  1. The first version of Studio Player, the player for listening to and studying music, fully accessible with the screen reader.
  2. Independent speed and pitch, the A-B loop with saved loops, the speeding-up loop and the loop sequence, markers.
  3. The metronome, on its own or on the song's beats, with a count-in before starting.
  4. The chords and key of the song, scales for improvising and the notes of the stems.
  5. Stem separation (vocals, guitar, bass, drums and the rest) with the mixer.
  6. MIDI files with sound banks, the channel mixer, instruments to change and karaoke.
  7. The same volume for all songs, parametric equalizer, stereo and channels, night listening.
  8. Videos in the player window, with the audio track, subtitles read aloud and chapters.
  9. The format converter and the export of the song as you hear it, also each stem in its own file.
  10. Automatic updates, the backup of the settings and the complete guide in the Help menu.
