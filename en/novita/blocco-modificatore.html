What's new in NVDA Modifier Lock

Versions from the most recent. What's new in the installed version can also be read in the NVDA add-on manager.

Back to NVDA Modifier Lock

Version 1.2.0

New

  1. The add-on speaks the language of NVDA: English or Italian.
  2. Guide in English and Italian.
  3. Package made like the official NVDA add-ons: translated name, description and changes, license.

Fixes

  1. The settings page opens correctly: before, it failed and showed the controls of other categories.
  2. Turning the lock on with NVDA+Control+Shift+L no longer locks the NVDA key by itself.
  3. With NVDA 2025.1 and later, keys sent by other programs no longer lock or unlock the NVDA key.

Version 1.1.0

New

  1. With NVDA 2025.1 and later, uses NVDA's official keyboard hook.
  2. Two quick taps of the NVDA key do its normal function again (for example Caps Lock).
  3. Beeps and messages no longer slow down the keyboard.
  4. Does nothing when Windows Sticky Keys are on.
  5. The lock is removed silently when switching window.
