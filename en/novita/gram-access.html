Version 2.1.7

Version 2.1.7 fixes a sudden closing of the program and updates the Telegram engine to the latest official version.

FIXES

  1. Sudden closing after sending a message. Sometimes, when pressing Enter, the message was sent and right after Gram Access closed by itself. The problem came from the program's sounds: when two sounds started very close together, for example a long message split into several parts, Windows could read a sound already deleted from memory. Now all sounds go through a single point and stay in memory as long as Windows is using them. The sounds are the same as always.

IMPROVEMENTS

  1. Telegram engine updated. The official Telegram library that Gram Access uses to talk to the servers (TDLib) moves to version 1.8.69. At the first start after the update Gram Access updates its data by itself and may take a few seconds longer than usual: there is no need to log in again.
  2. New message types read properly. Instead of a generic "[Attachment]" you now hear:
  • TON transfers, with the amount received or sent and the comment, if any;
  • TON Connect requests, with the app name and the state of the request (awaiting response, expired, accepted or rejected);
  • the notice that someone can message you without paying thanks to one of your links.
  1. TON addresses in rich messages. TON addresses inside rich messages are now read as normal text.

GOOD TO KNOW

  • Telegram introduced a new kind of proxy, the web proxy, which needs a hidden web page carrying the connection in order to work. Gram Access does not support it yet: in the proxy list it appears as "Web, not supported yet" and it can only be deleted. Using it would leave Gram Access without a connection, so a window explains this if you try to enable it, edit it or paste its link. SOCKS5, HTTP and MTProto proxies work as before.
  • All call features (1-to-1, group video calls, conference calls) remain EXPERIMENTAL: for any strange behavior, don't hesitate to contact me.

Version 2.1.5

Version 2.1.5 strengthens the protection of your data with two new options requested by users, and makes sure you do not miss the outcome of important actions.

NEW

  1. Local passcode and data protection. The item in the Telegram menu, Privacy submenu, is now called "Local Passcode and Data Protection..." and gathers all the protections. Without a passcode it offers "Create local passcode..." and "Bind data to this computer"; with the passcode, after typing it, you find "Change passcode", "Auto-lock if away for", "Log out after too many wrong attempts", "Bind data to this computer" and "Turn off passcode". Each item says its current state.
  2. Bind data to this computer. Windows keeps a secret that exists only in your user on this computer, and without that secret the Gram Access Telegram data (your account login and your chats) cannot be opened. If someone copies the Gram Access folder to another computer, the data does not open, not even with your local passcode, and trying random passcodes is useless. It works on its own, in which case Gram Access opens without asking anything but only here, or together with the local passcode: then both your passcode and your computer are needed, which is the strongest protection. Before turning it on, a read-only window explains everything calmly: what it protects against, what it does not, and what it means. Good to know: you will have to log in again with your phone number, losing secret chats and downloaded files, if you open the folder on another computer (even from a USB stick), change computer, reinstall Windows or switch to another user, or if an administrator resets your Windows user's password; a normal password change is fine. To change computer without losing anything, just turn it off before moving the folder and turn it on again on the new computer. If the folder is opened on another computer, Gram Access says so clearly and lets you choose whether to log in again or close without touching anything.
  3. Log out after too many wrong attempts. With the local passcode on, you can choose that after 5, 10, 15 or 20 wrong attempts in a row Gram Access logs out of your account. With Gram Access open and locked, it ends the session on Telegram's servers too; at start the data is still locked and Gram Access can only delete it from this computer, and the session stays in the device list until you end it from your phone. In the last three attempts Gram Access warns you, the right passcode resets the count, and closing and reopening the program does not reset it. Before turning it on, a window explains everything. It is off at first: it protects against someone trying to guess the passcode on this computer, and if you are the one making mistakes you will have to log in again.

IMPROVEMENTS

  1. Outcomes of actions in a window. Many confirmations came only from the screen reader and were easily lost if speech interrupted them. Now they appear in a window: two-step verification (password set, changed or removed, recovery email confirmed), active sessions (session terminated or confirmed, all other sessions terminated), profile and settings changes in the Telegram menu, group and channel management (name, description, permissions, reactions, advanced settings, administrators, restrictions and more), proxies, close friends, reports and join requests. In addition, the confirmation arrives only after Telegram's real answer: before, some group actions said "updated" right away, even when Telegram then refused them.
  2. Go to menu, Folder submenu. The submenu was often empty and, leaving it with the left arrow, you went back to the first item of "Go to". Now it always contains "All chats" and your folders, and it appears only if you have at least one folder.

GOOD TO KNOW

  • None of these protections encrypts files already downloaded (photos, voice messages, documents) or call recordings: if you want to protect those too, keep Gram Access on an encrypted disk or USB stick. Everything is explained in the Guide (in Italian), section "Il codice di blocco e la protezione dei dati".
  • All call features (1-to-1, group video calls, conference calls) remain EXPERIMENTAL: for any strange behavior, don't hesitate to contact me.

Version 2.1.4

Version 2.1.4 brings the local passcode, the same as in the official Telegram for computers: it protects your account if someone copies the Gram Access folder.

NEW

  1. Local passcode. Gram Access is portable and everything needed to get into your account is in its Dati folder: without protection, anyone who copies it to another computer can use your account. Now you can set a passcode from the Telegram menu, Privacy submenu, "Local Passcode...". Gram Access asks for it at every start and uses it to encrypt your account login and the chats stored on the computer: without the passcode nobody can get into your account or read your chats. The passcode is never saved. From the same item you can change it or turn it off. After three wrong attempts you have to wait a few seconds before trying again. It is optional: if you do not set it, Gram Access works as before.
  2. Lock Gram Access. With the passcode on, you can lock Gram Access while it is open, from "Lock Gram Access" in the Telegram menu, or with a shortcut that you assign in Settings, Keyboard, and that works in any window of the program. All windows disappear and only the passcode field remains; when you unlock, every window comes back as it was. While locked, messages keep arriving, but notifications only say "You have a new message" and the screen reader announces nothing from your chats. Calls work as usual: you can answer even while locked.
  3. Auto-lock. In the passcode settings you choose after how long without using the computer Gram Access locks itself: 1 minute, 5 minutes, 1 hour, 5 hours, or your own time in hours and minutes. It starts at 1 hour, as in the official Telegram.
  4. If you forget the passcode. It cannot be recovered, which is what makes it safe, but you can always get back into your account with the "I forgot the passcode..." button: Gram Access deletes the Telegram data on the computer and you log in again with your phone number. All normal chats come back, secret chats and downloaded files are lost, Gram Access settings are kept.

GOOD TO KNOW

  • The passcode protects your account login and your chats. Files already downloaded (photos, voice messages, documents), call recordings and Gram Access settings remain readable: if you want to protect those too, keep Gram Access on an encrypted disk or USB stick. Everything is explained in the Guide (in Italian), section "Il codice di blocco locale".
  • All call features (1-to-1, group video calls, conference calls) remain EXPERIMENTAL: for any strange behavior, don't hesitate to contact me.

Version 2.1.3

Version 2.1.3 makes Gram Access's automatic updates safer. The protections apply from the next update onward, that is, when you move from this version to the following one.

IMPROVEMENTS

  1. Signed updates. Before installing an update, Gram Access checks that the downloaded package is exactly the one published and signed by the author. If even a single byte is different, because of a damaged download or a tampered file, it installs nothing and tells you so in a window, offering to open the Gram Access page on the website to download the latest version. The same happens if the update is being published at that very moment: just try again in a few minutes.
  2. "All or nothing" updates. If even one file cannot be replaced, for example because another program has locked it, all the previous files are put back and Gram Access reopens as it was, telling you so in a window. A half-updated program can no longer happen. The Dati folder, with your Telegram login and your settings, is never touched.

Version 2.1.1

Version 2.1.1 brings call recording: Telegram's own recording, now complete as in the official client, and a new recording on your computer, with a separate track for each voice.

NEW

  1. Recording on the computer with separate tracks. An extra Gram Access feature that Telegram doesn't have: it works in every call (one-to-one, group video chats and conferences) and you don't need to be an admin. During a call press the "Record on Computer" button or Alt+Shift+G; to stop it, "Stop Recording" or Alt+Shift+G again. It also stops by itself when the call ends. Each voice goes into its own file: yours ("Me (microphone)"), the audio of your shared screen ("Me (my screen audio)"), each participant's and, in video chats and conferences, the shared screen audio of whoever is sharing; plus, if you want, an "Everyone together" file. All tracks start at the same moment: whoever joins later has silence until they arrive, so they line up in an editing program. While you are muted your track records silence. In the folder you also find "Information.txt", with date, duration, files and at which minute each person was present or left. At the end the program tells you how many files it saved and offers to open the folder. In one-to-one calls the other person has a single track, because their voice and their screen audio already arrive together.
  2. Recording settings. In Settings, Calls category: the format (WAV, full quality, or MP3, small files), the "Everyone together" file, the reminder and the folder. At first the folder is Dati\Registrazioni, inside the Gram Access folder, which updates never touch. The other participants don't see any notice of this recording: that's why Gram Access reminds you to tell them when you start (the reminder can be turned off).
  3. Telegram recording notice. Gram Access now tells you when a video chat is being recorded by Telegram: as soon as you join, when someone starts or stops the recording, and Control+Shift+I repeats it. Before, it never said so, while people using official Telegram always see it.

IMPROVEMENTS

  1. Telegram recording as in the official client. In "Video Chat Settings...", the "Start recording..." item lets you choose what to record (audio only, or also the video in landscape or portrait) and then the title, already filled in with the video chat name; stopping it asks for confirmation. The announcement arrives only after Telegram's answer, and at the end it tells you the file is in Saved Messages. The item says "Start" or "Stop" according to the real state, even if another admin started the recording or you manage the video chat without joining it.

GOOD TO KNOW

  • For MP3, Gram Access uses the LAME library (LGPL license), included in the program; details are in the "About the program" window.
  • All call features (1-to-1, group video calls, conference calls) remain EXPERIMENTAL: for any strange behavior, don't hesitate to contact me.

Version 2.1.0

Version 2.1.0 is dedicated to calls: a thorough check of all call engines, compared with Telegram Desktop, found and fixed the causes of the most serious problems with conferences and screen sharing, and brings the participant menu up to the level of Telegram.

NEW

  1. Full participant menu. In group video chats and conferences, the Applications key on the selected person opens the same menu as Telegram: "Mute for everyone" and "Allow to unmute" if you manage the call, otherwise "Mute for me" and "Unmute for me", which only apply to you; then the volume, "Open profile", "Send message" and, in conferences you created, "Remove from call". The items always act on the person you opened the menu on, even if the list changes in the meantime.
  2. Invited people in conferences. People you invited who haven't joined yet appear in the list marked "invited". From their menu you can cancel the invitation; if they decline or don't answer, the program tells you and the row disappears.
  3. Webcam in conferences too. The video button is now available in conferences as well, as in Telegram.
  4. The other person's state in one-to-one calls. You are told when the other person turns their microphone off or back on and when their battery is low, as in Telegram Desktop; Control+Shift+I repeats it.
  5. Muted by an admin. If an admin mutes you, your microphone really turns off (before, only the icon seen by others changed), webcam and screen sharing turn off and you hear "Muted by admin". When you are allowed to speak again you hear "You can now speak", but the microphone stays off until you unmute yourself, as in Telegram.

FIXES

  1. Screen sharing in a conference. As soon as sharing started you could no longer hear the others, and they became audible again only when you stopped it. The cause: the screen connection also read the incoming audio, and Telegram discarded the same packets as "already processed" when they reached the main connection. Now the screen connection only sends, as in Telegram Desktop.
  2. People muted for the whole conference. If someone's audio arrived before Gram Access knew whose it was, that person stayed silent for the whole call, especially after an automatic reconnection. Now Gram Access waits and asks Telegram whose audio it is, and after a reconnection it immediately finds who was already there.
  3. Participants disappearing from the list. A muted person could disappear from the list, as if they had left, while still in the call. Now the participant list is loaded in full.
  4. Microphone after an automatic reconnection. If you were muted and the group call or conference reconnected by itself, the microphone came back on while the button still said "Unmute Microphone". Now you stay muted, and the button always tells the real state.
  5. Webcam in group video chats. When you turned on the webcam, Gram Access didn't tell Telegram, so others might not receive your video. Now it does, like Telegram Desktop.
  6. Participant volume. The volume chosen for a person also applies to the audio of their shared screen, and "Mute for me" really mutes their audio for you.
  7. "Speaking". Gram Access now also tells Telegram when the other participants speak, like Unigram, so the "speaking" indication in the list is more reliable.
  8. Call window. In conferences the "Add Participant..." and "Video Chat Settings..." buttons were on top of each other, and the participant list was partly covered by the buttons.

GOOD TO KNOW

  • These fixes come from the problems seen in real tests, but they still need to be tried in calls with several people: if something doesn't work, please report it, with the time it happened.
  • All call features (1-to-1, group video calls, conference calls) remain EXPERIMENTAL: for any strange behavior, don't hesitate to contact me.

Version 2.0.8

Version 2.0.8 brings Telegram comments to Gram Access: reading and writing comments on channel posts, threads in groups and, for channel administrators, managing the discussion group.

NEW

  1. Comments on channel posts. A channel can have a discussion group: every post also arrives there, and each post can be commented on. The post line now says "12 comments", "1 comment" or "Leave a comment" if nobody has written yet, as in Telegram. To open them press Control+Shift+J on the post, or choose "Comments" (or "Leave a comment") in the message menu. On a text-only post Enter is enough; if the post also has links, buttons or an attachment, the "Comments" item is in the Enter menu ("Read full message..." stays on Control+Enter). The list shows only that post and its comments: you start from the first unread comment, or from the last one, and going back up to the top you find the channel post. Esc takes you back to the channel, on the post you started from.
  2. Writing a comment. Inside the comments the writing field comments on that post: you can also reply to a single comment, react, send voice messages and attachments. As in Telegram you don't need to join the discussion group, unless the group only lets members write. New comments arrive while you read, and the comment count on the post line updates by itself.
  3. Threads in groups. In discussion groups, on messages that received replies, the line says "8 replies" and the same item is called "View 8 replies": it works like comments, with the same shortcut and the same Enter. Esc goes back to the whole group chat, on the message you started from.
  4. View discussion. In a channel with comments, the Group menu has the "View discussion" item, which opens the discussion group with all its messages.
  5. Discussion group for administrators. In the Administration menu, under Advanced Settings, a channel's "Discussion group..." item tells you which group is linked, with the "Unlink group" button. If there isn't one you find the list of your suitable groups, each with its "@name" or "private group", and at the top "Create a new group", which creates a group named like the channel plus "Chat" (the name can be changed) and links it. When you choose a group, Gram Access asks for confirmation with the same warnings as Telegram: who will be able to see the messages and, if needed, that the chat history will become visible to new members. A normal group is first turned into a supergroup. Inside a linked group the item is called "Linked channel..." and lets you unlink it. The outcome always arrives in a window, after Telegram's answer.

IMPROVEMENTS

  1. "Typing" in the right place. While you write inside a forum topic or inside comments, the others see "typing" right there, as in Telegram, and no longer in the general group chat.
  2. Number of replies. With a single reply the line now says "1 reply" instead of "1 replies". In "Customize Display" the item is now called "Number of replies and comments".

FIXES

  1. "Linked Discussion Group..." never showed the list of groups to link, and inside a group it sent the wrong request to Telegram.

Version 2.0.7

Version 2.0.7 completes the Enter key on messages: it now always does something useful, whatever the message. Plus, a shortcut to translate and a fix for choosing members.

NEW

  1. Enter is never silent. On a message without an action of its own, for example a plain text, Enter opens "Read full message...", where you read the message calmly, line by line.
  2. One menu for everything. When a message contains several things to do, Enter gathers them in a single menu: first the message's buttons (for example "Go to offer" in channel posts), then "Read full message...", then an "Open link" for each link in the text, and finally the actions on the attachment. This happens with photos and GIFs, with an attachment that has a link in its caption, with a text that has several links and with a message that has buttons together with links or attachments. On a photo you find "Describe image", "Read text in image", "Open with the Windows program" and "Save as...". A link written as a word shows the word and, in brackets, the address. A message with buttons only, like a bot's menus, opens the buttons directly as before.
  3. Special content. As in Telegram, Enter on a poll opens the window to vote, or the results if you have already voted or it is closed; on a checklist it opens the checklist; on a location or a place it opens the map in the browser; on a contact it opens the chat with that person, or offers "Send a message" and "Add to Contacts" if they aren't in your contacts yet; on a sticker it opens its pack, offering to add it.
  4. Service messages. Enter on "pinned a message" takes you to the pinned message; on a video chat that started, is scheduled or on an invitation, if it is still going on, it asks whether you want to join; on "changed the photo" it opens the photo menu (Describe image included); on a person who joined, was added or removed it opens their Info card.
  5. Control+Enter and Shift+Enter. On the message list, Control+Enter always opens "Read full message...". Shift+Enter takes you to the origin of the message: for a reply the message it replies to, for a forwarded message its source (the original message, the channel post or the person's card), for "pinned a message" the pinned message. On forwarded and pinned messages the same action is also in the message menu, "Go to origin". Both combinations can be changed in Settings, Keyboard.
  6. Control+Alt+T translates the selected message, like "Translate to English" in the message menu. On a message without text, like a photo without a caption, Gram Access tells you there is nothing to translate.

IMPROVEMENTS

  1. "Read full message..." with the translation. If the message has been translated, the window shows both the translation and the original text, in the same order as the message line chosen in "Customize Display".

FIXES

  1. When choosing members ("New group", "Add Members", "Close friends..." and bot requests), people already checked were removed from the selection every time you searched for another person, and in "Close friends..." the ones chosen before didn't show as checked. Now they all stay.
  2. "Read full message..." only showed the automatic translation, never the one requested from the message menu.

Version 2.0.5

Version 2.0.5 is dedicated to bots: they become simple to use even for those who don't know them, and as complete as in Telegram. Plus, long texts can be sent without splitting them by hand.

NEW

  1. Starting a new bot. Opening a bot you have never used shows the "What can this bot do?" window, with the description in a field you can read calmly with the arrow keys and the "Start the bot" and "Not now" buttons. Until you start it, the Send button is called "Start the bot" and Enter in the empty field is enough; the same for a bot you had stopped.
  2. Telegram links inside Gram Access. t.me links, in messages and in bot buttons, no longer go to the browser: a link to a person, a bot, a group or a channel opens its chat; an invite shows where you are joining and asks for confirmation; a link to a message takes you to that message; a link that starts a bot with a code opens the bot with its description. The "Add me to a group" button of management bots lets you choose the group among the suitable ones and, if the bot wants to be an administrator, lists the permissions it asks for before you confirm.
  3. Telegram links from outside. With the "Windows integration" checkbox in General settings, tg:// links opened from a web page or another program also reach Gram Access, whether it is already open or not. For safety, a link coming from outside never starts a bot by itself.
  4. Bot buttons and keyboard. When reading a message you hear "Buttons:" with their names, or "Bot keyboard" when the bot has shown its keys in place of the normal keyboard. The bot keyboard opens from anywhere in the chat, even while you type, with Control+Shift+B, or from the Contact or Group menu. The reading item can be removed from "Customize Display".
  5. Bot requests. The keys with which a bot asks for your phone number, to create a poll, to choose one or more people, a group or a channel now work, always with a clear confirmation before sending anything of yours; for groups and channels you are told whether the bot will be added and with which permissions. The location can't be shared from a computer, not even with Telegram Desktop: Gram Access tells you so.
  6. Commands with "/". Typing "/" at the start of the message, in a chat with a bot or in a group with bots, you hear a tone: Down Arrow opens the list of commands with their descriptions, filtered by the letters you type. Enter sends the command, Tab writes it in the field to complete it, Escape cancels. In a group with several bots, the command goes to the right bot.
  7. Inline bots. They are used inside any chat: type for example "@lybot" and the title of a song, press Down Arrow and choose the result to send, read for what it is (article, photo, GIF, video, audio...). If you forget the space after the name, Gram Access adds it by itself. Alternatively, Control+Shift+K or "Inline bot..." in the Attach menu opens a window with the recently used bots, a search that updates as you type and the list of results.
  8. "Share" and "Log in" buttons. A "Share" button prepares the bot's search in the field, in the open chat or in a chat you choose among the suitable ones. A "Log in" button opens a website logging in with your Telegram account, after asking whether you want to log in or open the site without logging in, and whether the bot may write to you.
  9. Bots that answer live. When an artificial intelligence bot writes its answer while composing it, Gram Access tells you once; you can listen to the text written so far with Control+Shift+S. If the bot allows it, the Send button becomes "Stop the bot's reply".
  10. "Only for you" commands in groups. Some bot commands go privately to the bot and the other members don't see them: Gram Access notices by itself and tells you. In the Group menu, "Bot Commands..." lists the commands of the group's bots.
  11. Messages updated only for you. When a bot changes one of its messages only for you, you hear "Updated for you" and in the message menu you find "Revert", which brings back the original message.
  12. The bot is waiting for a reply. When a bot asks a question and wants the answer linked to that message, Gram Access sets the reply by itself and tells you, without moving your cursor.
  13. Long texts. The message field no longer has the limit of about 4000 characters: you can paste even a very long text, and when sending, Gram Access splits it by itself into several messages, like official Telegram, cutting between paragraphs and keeping the formatting. After sending you hear how many messages it was sent as. With Premium, if Telegram allows it for the account, each message can be longer.

  1. More complete rich messages. Expandable quotes, documents (which can be opened and saved from the message's content list) and the buttons inside the message, pressed from "Message buttons", are now read too; a part this version can't read is indicated instead of disappearing.
  2. A button disabled by the bot appears grayed out. The text accompanying a gift is read, and someone joining a group from a community is indicated as such.
  3. Guide. New "First steps with bots" section, in seven simple steps, and the description of all the new features.

  2. The message field silently cut a pasted text beyond 4096 characters.
  3. In the Contact menu of a bot never started, "Stop Bot" appeared instead of "Start Bot".
