Studio Player

Listening to and studying music with your screen reader: slowing a song down without changing its pitch, repeating a passage, separating the instruments and finding the chords.

Studio Player is a player designed for people who play and study music. Open a song and you can slow it down without changing its pitch, change the pitch without changing the speed, repeat a difficult passage as many times as you like with the A-B loop and mark the sections with markers. Everything is controlled with the keyboard, and your screen reader always tells you where you are and what is happening.

When you want to go deeper, Studio Player separates a song into its parts (vocals, guitar, bass, drums and the rest): you can listen to the guitar alone to understand what it plays, or remove it and play along yourself. It recognizes the chords and the key, suggests scales for improvising, finds the notes of the stems and plays MIDI files with the sound banks you prefer.

And when it is time to play, Studio Player works with professional audio interfaces through their ASIO® drivers: you hear your instrument in the headphones together with the song, record yourself over the song as many times as you want and compare the takes on the fly, and send the metronome and every part of the song to its own outputs, for example only the click in your headphones when you play live. MIDI files can also play on your external sound module.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • A screen reader: Studio Player talks with NVDA and JAWS even when it is not in the foreground.
  • For stems, chords and notes: the stem engine, downloaded separately from the Settings because it is large. With an NVIDIA graphics card it is much faster.
  • For instrument monitoring, recordings and separate outputs: an audio interface with ASIO® drivers.

What it does

  • Independent speed and pitch: slow down without changing the pitch, change the pitch without changing the speed.
  • The A-B loop, saved loops for each song, the loop that speeds up a little at every pass and the loop sequence, with a pause and a count-in between passes.
  • Named markers, to jump straight to the verse or the chorus.
  • The metronome, on its own or on the song's beats, with a count-in before starting.
  • Stems: vocals, guitar, bass, drums (also split into its pieces), piano and the rest, with a mixer for every part; also for a whole playlist at once, one song after another.
  • The lyrics aligner: it re-times on the singer's voice the lyrics found on the internet, even when they come from another recording of the song, and prepares stems and timed lyrics for a whole folder for studying, without going through karaoke.
  • The lyrics editor: write, fix and time the lyrics of a track by hand, tapping the time by ear or with the help of the aligner, and hear the result at once.
  • The MIDI editor for playing live: in every track, moments on a bar and beat or at a precise time send Program Changes, Control Changes and notes to another program, for example Reaper, in the playlist and in karaoke.
  • The chords and key of the song, also spoken while it plays, and scales for improvising.
  • The notes of the stems, to understand a melody or a bass line.
  • MIDI files with SoundFont sound banks or on your external sound module, the channel mixer, instruments to change and karaoke.
  • Guitar Pro tablature and MusicXML scores, read as string and fret or as notes, even one piece at a time, with tracks, bars and listening to every passage.
  • The tuner for guitar, bass and ukulele, with a tick that guides you to the right note and many tunings.
  • The same volume for all songs, parametric equalizer, stereo and channels, night listening.
  • Videos in the player window, with the choice of audio track, subtitles read aloud and chapters as markers.
  • The format converter and the export of the song as you hear it: with the chosen stems, speed, pitch and effects, the whole song or just a section.
  • Audio interfaces with ASIO® drivers: music on the outputs you choose and your instrument from an input, in mono or stereo, also to REAPER with ReaRoute, with instrument monitoring in the headphones.
  • Recordings over the song, aligned by themselves: single keys to record, compare on the fly, pick the best one and export it together with the song; with the loop, one pass only or a recording for every pass.
  • Separate outputs for playing live: the metronome and count-in only in the headphones, and every stem or MIDI channel on its own output of the interface.
  • Playlists: add songs to a saved playlist from the context menu, without opening it, export it as text to print or copy it with its songs to a USB stick; and the Properties of every song, with all its data and the lyrics, if there are any.
  • Announcements by voice or with recognizable sounds, fully customizable shortcuts, also working everywhere in Windows, and automatic updates, in Italian, English and Spanish.
