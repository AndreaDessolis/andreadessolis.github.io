Hi, I'm Andrea

Music, technology and the wish to make things a little easier to use, for everyone.

This is my small place online. Here you find the programs I build, all free and designed from the start for people who use a screen reader, some NVDA add-ons and, if you like, my story on the "About me" page.

L'angolo di HDX

The Telegram group for all my programs and add-ons: news, questions, bug reports and suggestions, together with other users. The group is mostly in Italian, but you can write in your own language: translators make it easy to understand each other.
